Our Philosophy

Where Steam Meets Spirit

In the Latvian tradition, the pirts is far more than a sauna—it is a sacred space where physical, emotional, and spiritual purification converge. For centuries, our ancestors understood that true wellness embraces body, mind, and soul as one.

The Latvian word "garīgais" carries a profound duality: it means both "steam" and "spirit." This ancient wisdom reminds us that in the gentle embrace of warmth and vapor, we find not only physical cleansing but spiritual renewal.

"In the pirts, we do not simply bathe—we are reborn."

The Ritual

Traditional Elements of the Pirts

Each session honors the ancient practices passed down through generations of Latvian pirts masters.

Birch Whisks

The slota—bundles of birch branches—gently stimulate circulation and release tension, connecting you to the forest's healing essence.

Herbal Infusions

Steam infused with wild herbs—chamomile, mint, juniper—carries the healing spirits of Latvian meadows into every breath.

Honey Treatments

Local honey, nature's golden elixir, nourishes and purifies the skin while honoring the ancient bond between bees and Baltic people.
